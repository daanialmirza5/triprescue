SAY:

"Hello everyone, good [morning/afternoon]. Thank you for the opportunity to present our project — TripRescue.

Before I show you anything, let me start with the problem, because that's really the whole reason this project exists.

Imagine you're a traveler on a multi-leg trip. You fly Mumbai to Delhi, connect to a flight to Leh, take a ground transfer to your hotel, and then you have a couple of days of activities booked — say, a Pangong Lake tour and a Nubra Valley excursion — before flying back. That's eight separate bookings that all depend on each other happening roughly on time and in order.

Now, your very first flight — Mumbai to Delhi — gets delayed by three hours. What happens? In most travel apps, absolutely nothing happens on the app's side. The app just shows you 'your flight is delayed.' It doesn't tell you that this delay might cause you to miss your connecting flight to Leh. It doesn't tell you that if you miss that connection, your prebooked transfer to the hotel is now booked for a time you'll never arrive at. It doesn't tell you your hotel check-in is now at risk, or that the activities you booked assumed you'd already be in Leh by a certain time.

That is the real problem: disruptions in travel are never isolated. They cascade. And almost no existing tool actually reasons about that cascade — they track individual bookings, not the relationships between them.

So, what is TripRescue? TripRescue is an explainable travel disruption recovery engine for multi-leg itineraries. And I want to stress the word 'explainable,' because that's the core design philosophy here. It doesn't just tell you something is broken — it tells you exactly why it's broken, in terms of real numbers. It then generates multiple feasible ways to recover, ranks those ways according to what YOU actually care about — cost, speed, comfort — lets you apply one, and then re-checks the entire trip again to make sure that fix didn't quietly create a new problem somewhere else.

Let me show you exactly how that works, live."
